w1.3: One thing I still do not understand is how we are supposed to compare two regions when they collect their census data in completely different ways?

w4.1: We had the first joint seminar with the other group today. It was much less awkward than I had built it up to be. One of them had already read the book I was struggling with and explained the argument better than the lecture did.

w4.2: I noticed that I kept waiting to be asked for my opinion, while the others simply offered theirs. I had assumed that was politeness on my part, and then realised it just looked like I had nothing to say. I started speaking earlier in the discussion after that.

w7.1: The field week was the best part of the course by a long way. Working out a land-use survey with people who learned geography under a different curriculum meant we argued for an hour about what counted as urban before we could begin. That argument taught me more than the reading had.

w7.2: Long week. Good though.

w10.1: I have signed up for the semester abroad, which I would have called impossible back in September. I was hesitant at the very start of this course and I am now the person telling everyone else to choose the field option.

w10.2: I am still a quiet person in groups and I do not think that part changes. What changed is that I stopped reading my own silence as a failure, and I now ask for the floor when I actually need it.
