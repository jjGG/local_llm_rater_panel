Reflection journal — marine field course

Week 1
• What are you expecting from the field course?
Mostly I want to get better at identifying algae. The boat leaves at six every morning, which is brutal. I am sharing a room with someone from the other partner group and I am genuinely looking forward to that.

• What worries you?
My swimming is weak. I also get seasick, which is an awkward problem on a field course about the sea.

Week 4
• How is the group working together?
The sampling is going fine. We had a real argument about protocol on day three. I have learned that the two labs teach completely different rules for what counts as a replicate, so both of us had been right by our own standards. We rewrote the protocol sheet the same evening.

• Anything difficult?
The heat, mainly. And the wifi, which means I cannot upload anything until we are back in port.

Week 7
• Has your own behaviour changed?
I now ask people to repeat the plan back to me instead of assuming we agree. I also realised I had been reading the quieter members of the group as uninterested, when they were being polite in a way I am not used to. My algae identification is still poor.

• What about the science itself?
The transect data looks good. We lost one quadrat to a wave, which I am still annoyed about.

Week 10
• What will you take away from the course?
A lot of practical skill with a dredge. Also that I am someone who assumes everyone works the way I do, which I did not know about myself before this course. The final report is due on Friday and I have not started it.

• Would you do it again?
Yes. Not in August.
