Reflection journal — food product development project

Week 1
• What are you looking forward to?
Honestly, the sensory panel sessions. I have wanted to do proper trained-panel work since first year. I am also curious to find out what the team at the other university considers a normal breakfast, because that is half of our brief.

• Any concerns?
My lab maths is shaky. And I always talk too much in meetings.

Week 4
• How is the project going?
We have a product concept and a very messy Gantt chart. What counts as too salty is simply different in the two countries, which I had never thought about before. We lost a week arguing past each other before anyone worked that out. The texture analyser was broken again.

• Anything you would change about the set-up?
More lab time. The seminar room is too small for eight people.

Week 7
• Have you changed how you work with the team?
I have learned to let people finish, especially the two who are working in their third language. I have also started writing quantities in both units, because assuming that everyone thinks in grams was arrogant of me. The sensory panel results are beautiful.

• Anything else?
I miss the good coffee machine on the third floor.

Week 10
• What are you taking away from the project?
A genuine skill with descriptive analysis. Also the knowledge that I am the kind of person who assumes their own habits are the default, which is uncomfortable but useful. I would work with this team again tomorrow.

• Anything for the course team?
Fix the texture analyser. Otherwise it was the best project I have done.
